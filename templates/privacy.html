Privacy notice

Grief Tracker — logistics issue tracking system

This notice explains what personal data we hold about you when you use Grief Tracker, why we hold it, how long we keep it, and how you can exercise your rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. What we collect

  • Account data: email, username, optional first/last name, optional department, role.
  • Authentication data: hashed password, last login timestamp, JWT refresh-token records (for revocation).
  • Operational data: grief issues you create or edit are linked to your account so the audit trail shows who did what.
  • Technical logs: server logs may include IP addresses and user-agent strings for security purposes (rate-limiting, abuse detection).

2. Why we hold it (lawful basis)

  • Contract — we need account data to provide you the service.
  • Legitimate interest — audit logs and rate-limit logs help us keep the system secure.
  • Consent — we ask you to accept this notice at registration. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

3. How long we keep it

Account and operational data are kept for as long as your account is active. If you erase your account (Art. 17, see below), your personal fields (email, name, department) are anonymised immediately; the underlying issue records remain because they form part of business records, but they are no longer linked to identifiable PII.

Server logs are kept for 30 days then rotated.

4. Your rights

  • Access (Art. 15) — GET /api/v1/auth/me/
  • Rectification (Art. 16) — PATCH /api/v1/auth/me/
  • Erasure (Art. 17) — DELETE /api/v1/auth/me/delete/ — anonymises your account immediately.
  • Data portability (Art. 20) — GET /api/v1/auth/me/export/ returns a JSON dump of every field we hold about you.
  • Withdraw consent — deleting your account also withdraws the consent recorded at registration.
  • Lodge a complaint — you have the right to complain to your national data-protection authority.

5. Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties beyond the infrastructure providers needed to host the service (database, hosting, TLS termination). We do not sell or rent your data.

6. Security

  • Passwords are stored using PBKDF2 with a per-user salt (Django default).
  • Authentication uses short-lived JWTs (1h) with rotating refresh tokens and a server-side blacklist.
  • Login and registration are rate-limited.
  • Production deployments use HTTPS-only cookies, HSTS, and strict CORS/CSRF.
